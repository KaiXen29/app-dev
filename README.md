# My Favorite Movie

## The Amazing Spider-Man (2012)

**The Amazing Spider-Man** is my favorite superhero movie.

It follows **Peter Parker**, a high school student who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider. He uses his powers to fight crime and protect the people of New York City.

### Why I Like It
- The action scenes are exciting and entertaining.
- Spider-Man uses his powers to help and protect people.
- Peter Parker is smart, brave, and determined.
- The movie shows the importance of responsibility and courage.

### My Favorite Character
My favorite character is **Peter Parker / Spider-Man** because he is *brave, intelligent, and responsible*.

### What I Learned
The movie taught me that **having great power comes with great responsibility**. We should use our abilities to help others, stand up for what is right, and never give up even when life becomes difficult.
